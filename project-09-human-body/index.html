Explore / 01 · Circulatory system

The body's delivery network.

Your heart drives blood through a branching network of vessels. Blood carries oxygen and nutrients to tissues and transports carbon dioxide and other waste products away.

Explore / 02 · Respiratory system

Every breath is a gas exchange.

Your diaphragm and rib muscles change the volume of your chest. Air moves into the lungs, where oxygen diffuses into blood and carbon dioxide moves out to be exhaled.

Breathing simulator

Take a breath.

Move the breathing rate slider. The lung illustration expands and contracts to represent a breathing cycle. Inhalation draws air in; exhalation moves air out.

From lunch to usable fuel.

Digestion breaks food into smaller molecules. Nutrients are absorbed mainly in the small intestine and transported around the body to be used, stored or processed.

Explore / 04 · The bigger picture

Many systems. One organism.

Body systems are not independent machines. They coordinate continuously to maintain stable internal conditions — a process called homeostasis.

🧠

Nervous system

Uses electrical and chemical signals to sense the environment, coordinate movement and regulate organs.

🦴

Skeletal system

Supports the body, protects organs, stores minerals and works with muscles to create movement.

💪

Muscular system

Skeletal, smooth and cardiac muscles create movement, move materials through organs and pump blood.

🛡️

Immune system

Recognises and helps defend against pathogens and abnormal cells using cells, tissues and signalling molecules.

⚖️

Endocrine system

Hormones act as chemical messengers that help regulate growth, metabolism, reproduction and stress responses.

🫘

Urinary system

Kidneys filter blood, regulate water and electrolyte balance, and produce urine to remove dissolved waste.
