From data to answer

Think of AI as a pipeline. Each stage transforms information until the system can produce a useful output.

1. Data

Models learn from examples. Text, images, audio, numbers and other information are converted into representations a computer can process.

2. Training

During training, a model repeatedly compares its predictions with known examples and adjusts internal parameters to reduce its errors.

3. Inference

Once trained, the model can process new input. It uses the patterns encoded in its parameters to calculate likely outputs.
