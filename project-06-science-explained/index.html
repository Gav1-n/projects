Experiment 01 / Mechanics

Why do things fall?

Gravity accelerates objects toward Earth. Change the drop height and watch the fall time change. In this idealised model, air resistance is ignored.

Drop test · Earth gravity
01 — GRAVITY

Free fall, in action.

Near Earth's surface, a falling object accelerates at about 9.8 metres per second squared. Its speed increases as it falls.

Fall time 2.02 s
Impact speed 19.8 m/s

What is a wave?

Waves transfer energy. Adjust frequency and amplitude to see how the shape changes. The wave shown is a simplified transverse wave.

02 — WAVES

Energy on the move.

Amplitude is the maximum displacement from the middle. Frequency is how many cycles pass a point each second.

Experiment 03 / Optics

Why does light bend?

When light enters a different material, its speed changes. This can change its direction too — a phenomenon called refraction.

Light entering water
03 — LIGHT

Change the angle.

Try changing the incoming angle. The ray bends toward the normal as it enters water from air because light travels more slowly in water.

Refracted angle 32.1°
Material Air → water
